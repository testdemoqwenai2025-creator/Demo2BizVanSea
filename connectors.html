🛡️ Ethical Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

  • ✓ Business contact info only (registered agent, business address, public filing contacts)
  • ✗ No individual PII (personal emails, home addresses, phone numbers)
  • ✗ No voter registration data (even if public — weaponization risk > commercial value)
  • ✗ No scraping private platforms (LinkedIn, Crunchbase) for contact info
  • ✓ Provenance for every record (source API, timestamp, SHA-256 hash)
  • ✓ Opt-out mechanism (any business can request removal)
  • ✓ Indigenous data sovereignty (nation controls their data)
