## What Is AIBizVan360?

AIBizVan360 is a comprehensive regional intelligence platform that transforms fragmented public data across the US-Canada Pacific Northwest corridor into actionable business insights. It covers:

- **14 intelligence verticals**: Government, Technology, Economics, Environment & Energy, Sports, Demographics, Transportation, Resources, Education, Leisure, Travel, and more
- **44 web pages** with 64,000+ lines of curated intelligence content
- **Multi-model AI chat** that classifies queries across 13 domains and delivers contextual responses
- **30 city-specific intelligence profiles** with AI Opportunity Scores and strategic assessments
- **Subscription monetization** (Explorer Free / Corridor Pass / Premium)

### Key Features

- Energy capacity intelligence panels with research papers, active projects, and AI insights
- Franchise valuation deep-dives with AI financial analysis
- 7-year energy forecast matrices with neural network projections
- Probabilistic project pipelines with Bayesian estimation
- Expandable card system with 5-8 tabbed deep-dives per card
- Cross-border trade flow visualization
- Dark/light themes, mobile responsive
- Live data refresh indicators with source attribution
