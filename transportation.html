Cascadia High-Speed Rail

Vancouver–Seattle–Portland: the transformational 250mph corridor

TIMELINE
Project Status & Milestones
$50M federal grant secured for environmental review and preliminary engineering. Business case targets 1-hour Vancouver-Seattle travel time. The bi-state Cascadia Rail Authority established by WA and OR legislatures. Construction could begin 2028 with service by 2035. Estimated cost: $24–$42B. Projected 3M annual riders by 2040.
ECONOMIC
Economic Impact Assessment
AI-modeled GDP impact: +$18.4B cumulative by 2045. Labor market effect: 120,000 job-years of construction employment, 8,400 permanent operational jobs. Property value uplift: +12% average within 2km of stations. Cross-border integration: reduces effective economic distance by 67%. Commuter shed expansion: 4.2M additional workers within 90-min reach.
CHALLENGES
Key Hurdles & Risk Factors
Cross-border customs integration remains the largest policy barrier. Geotechnical challenges in Fraser Valley and Cascade Range. Right-of-way acquisition in urban Seattle and Vancouver cores. Funding gap: $50M secured vs. $24B minimum required. Federal coordination: 4 agencies (US FRA, Transport Canada, WA DOT, BC MOTI). Political risk: bi-national project requires sustained bilateral commitment across election cycles.
